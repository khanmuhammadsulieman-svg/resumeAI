FAQ

Common questions

Is my data stored anywhere online? +

No — everything is saved to your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Can I export my data and move it elsewhere? +

Yes. Use Export JSON in Settings to download a full backup, and Import JSON to restore it.

Does the PDF actually preserve my template? +

Yes — the PDF export renders the exact template, fonts and colors you see in the live preview.

Can I build more than one resume? +

Yes — create, duplicate, rename and delete as many resumes as you like from your dashboard.
